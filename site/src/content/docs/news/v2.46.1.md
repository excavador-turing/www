In v2.46.0 the Repair button never appeared, because the board's root device shows as /dev/root and the daemon could not tell where it had booted from. Found on our own board right after flashing v2.46.0 and fixed the same day; the v2.46.0 post has the rest.

<!-- more -->

Firmware **v2.46.1**, 31 October 2026 — the first image to carry [bmcd 2.42.1](/changelog/bmcd/). Every item below is the repository's own changelog entry, taken apart; the whole entry is a click away under each one.

## Fixed

- **The settings-storage repair added in v2.46.0 is now offered on a real
board.** Bmcd 2.42.0 never offered it, because the board's root device shows up as `/dev/root` and bmcd could not tell what it was backed by (`backing: unknown`). bmcd 2.42.1 resolves it from the kernel's `root=` argument.

    <details class="note">
    <summary>The whole entry</summary>

    Nothing else changed.

    </details>

- **The settings-storage repair was never offered on a real board: the root
device shows as `/dev/root`, which 2.42.0 did not resolve.** On the board the kernel mounts the read-only image itself, so `/proc/mounts` says `/dev/root /mnt/rom erofs ...` and `/dev/root` is not a device node. <small class="tp-tag">bmcd 2.42.1</small>

    <details class="note">
    <summary>The whole entry</summary>

    `GET /api/bmc/storage` therefore reported `backing: "unknown"`, and with it `repairable: false`, even on a board that had lost its settings. When the mount source of the read-only root is `/dev/root` (or is not a device node at all, as for a root that is the overlay), the kernel's own `root=` from `/proc/cmdline` is resolved instead: a `MAJOR:MINOR` such as `254:0` through `/sys/class/block/*/dev` (which names it `ubiblock0_1`), or a `/dev/<name>` as it stands. `ubiblock*` is `ubi` and `mmcblk*` is `mmc`, as before. Whatever cannot be resolved (`PARTUUID=`, `PARTLABEL=`, a device sysfs does not list, no `root=`) stays `unknown`, and an unknown backing still does not offer the repair.

    </details>
